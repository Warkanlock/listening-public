Import your music

Import tagged MP3, AAC, M4A, and FLAC files. Choose a folder on your computer, an external drive, or a share your server can read. Listening imports a playable copy and can watch for changes.

Library search and playlists

Search by song, artist, album, or genre. Bring portable playlists, merge lists, and export tracks or playlists for other players. Connect Spotify to sync liked songs and playlists you own or collaborate on.

Playback and queue

Reorder the queue, play the next song, shuffle, or follow a genre. Supported players offer crossfade and volume normalization.

Device pairing and handoff

Pair a browser, phone, or computer. Choose playback devices and resume a library track at its saved position, including tracks from Library Radio.

Household accounts and Jams

Create separate profiles with their own favorites, playlists, queues, and history. Nearby Jams let listeners on the same network share a queue.

Artist and album pages

Open an artist or album from a song. Follow credits into other bands and solo projects, read release details, and play available tracks in album order.

Synced lyrics

Follow the current line when synced lyrics are available. Select a line to jump to that part of the song.

Radio and ambient sound

Shuffle your own songs by genre or play generated white or brown noise. Optional extensions can add other radio sources.

Mood Map, Deep Dive, and catalogs

Explore moods and related genres, follow weekly recommendations into discovery trails, or search public and configured private catalogs.

Offline listening and Camping mode

Buffer upcoming tracks for short interruptions. Save a mix of up to 30, 60, or 90 tracks for a trip. Android also supports offline sync, device files, and removable storage.

Playlist sharing and other players

Share a portable track list with a friend or publish it from a social account. Export original audio, make compact copies, or connect another compatible music player.

Requests, jobs, and extensions

Request a missing recording through a configured plugin. In Review copies, choose Stop searching to keep it out of automatic searches. The room verifies downloaded audio before it becomes playable. Compatible plugins can also share a selected folder.

Insights, storage, and backups

Check playback traffic, buffering, the upcoming queue, and free disk space. Back up the library and restore it on another server.

Set up a room.

Run Listening on one computer. Then open your room on the devices you listen on.

Mac

For Apple silicon Macs running macOS 13 or later.

  1. Open the Mac DMG. Drag Listening Room to Applications and open it.
  2. To join an existing room, enter its address or paste your invitation.
  3. To host your own, choose Listening Room → Install terminal command. Open Terminal, run ~/.local/bin/listening setup, and choose your music folder.
Download for Mac Mac setup guide

Linux server

  1. Install Docker with Compose and get the Listening release files.
  2. Follow the server guide to start a room, choose your music folder, and create your owner login.
  3. Open the address shown by setup. In your library, choose Owned music and play a track.
Illustrated setup guide Open this room’s setup

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open your room address or invitation in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open Listening from the new icon.

To keep a Camping mix offline, use a trusted HTTPS room and finish saving the mix from the Home Screen app.

iPhone and iPad guide

Android

  1. Download and open the Listening APK. Allow installation from your browser or Files app if Android asks.
  2. Open Listening and enter your room address or invitation. You can also open audio files already on the device.

Use Search to find songs, artists, or albums. Check for a new APK from Settings → App updates.

Download for Android Android guide

Browser on any computer

On Windows, Linux, macOS, or another device, open the room address or pairing invitation on the room’s private network. Sign in or pair when prompted.

Already have a room? Open Settings → Connect a device for its address, pairing, and any installers staged on that server.
